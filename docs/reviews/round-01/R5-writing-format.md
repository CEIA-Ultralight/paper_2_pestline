## 1. Paper Summary
The paper continues a data-centric pipeline for detecting and identifying four fly species (~40 px objects in 1920×1080 sticky-trap images) along two axes: (RQ1) three failure-targeted acquisition protocols (same-species crowding plates, graduated plates, blank negative plates) plus real-instance copy-paste; (RQ2) a hybrid $(1-w)\,\mathrm{CIoU}+w\,\mathrm{NWD}$ objective placed in both heads of an end-to-end YOLO26 (with GCD and RFLA variants), reporting a single-seed +1.4 pp VAL mAP50 and a collapse at $w{=}1$; (RQ3) a detector→crop→ConvNeXt-T-with-part-attention cascade that raises TEST species recall 75.7→80.1 % and F1 73.2→75.3 % while lowering 7-class mAP50 by 4.7 pp. The abstract additionally claims the cascade "disproves" the flesh-fly/blow-fly irreducibility statement of the prior PACBB 2026 paper. Evidence at this stage consists of Tab. 3 (`tab:rq3`, cascade on TEST), three single-seed rows of Tab. 2 (`tab:rq2`) and numbers quoted in the introduction; Tab. 1 (`tab:rq1`) is empty.

## 2. Strengths
1. **Honest, specific and unusually complete Limitations section (Sec. 6).** The draft states single-site/single-camera scope, the "consolidated, not blind" status of the test set, that the ensemble was chosen after seeing TEST, the seed-84 vs seed-42 spread (70.1–72.6 % F1), that two incompatible evaluators exist (0.857 vs 0.812 VAL mAP50 on the same E0 detector) and that the cascade latency is an upper bound. This is exactly what the CVPR reviewer guidelines ask reviewers to weigh positively, and it pre-empts the most common statistical objections.
2. **Clear three-RQ scaffold with an explicit thesis.** Sec. 1 P3 states the "data first, then the right model" thesis once and maps RQ1→Sec. 3, RQ2→Sec. 4/5.2, RQ3→Sec. 4.3/5.3. The contributions list (Sec. 1 P4) already refuses to claim RQ1 gains before the ablation exists, which is the right discipline.
3. **Related work is well positioned and differentiates from the prior paper in third person.** Sec. 2 covers all four required threads (AI-TOD/DotD/NWD/GCD/RFLA/QueryDet/SAHI/WBF; data-centric survey + Cut-Paste + Copy-Paste; MA-CNN/SupCon/ArcFace/ConvNeXt/Swin + detect-then-classify; Preti/IP102/YOLO-DCPG/YOLO-YSTs). The PACBB paper is cited as "[X] argued…", "its authors curated…", "they left three failure modes…" — consistently third person — and the delta (failure-targeted acquisition vs volume, objectives inside an NMS-free detector, cascade vs "irreducible") is stated in one sentence per paragraph.
4. **The "re-label but do not re-rank" explanation of the recall-up/mAP-down paradox (Sec. 1 contribution 3, Sec. 2 §3)** is a crisp mechanistic statement that turns a potentially embarrassing result into a methodological point about mAP as a deployment metric. Good argumentation.
5. **Bibliography hygiene.** 22/24 `.bib` entries carry DOIs and were reported as Crossref-verified; the five entries I spot-checked (NWD ISPRS 190:79–93; RFLA ECCV'22 LNCS 13669; QueryDet CVPR'22 pp. 13658–13667; SAHI ICIP'22 pp. 966–970; Zha et al. ACM CSUR 57(5) 2025) have plausible venues, years, pages and DOIs. No sign of hallucinated references.
6. **Prose quality.** The written sections are concise, jargon is mostly defined at first use (NWD, GCD, RFLA, WBF, DFL by expansion or formula), and the 0.1 %-of-the-image computation (44.4²/(1920·1080) ≈ 0.095 %) checks out.

## 3. Weaknesses

**Major (must fix before submission)**

1. **Overclaim in the abstract: "disproving the irreducibility claim" (sec/0_abstract.tex, last sentence) and "at fixed precision".** The only species-level evidence in `PAPER_FACTS.md` §3 is per-species P/R: flesh fly (MC) recall actually *drops* 85.3→81.2 % (precision 82.3→87.6 %), blow fly (MV) recall 83.6→86.9 %. That is a shift of the operating point between the two confusable classes, not a demonstration that the boundary is reducible; the intro itself admits (contribution 3, `\TODO`) that the pairwise flesh/blow confusion counts are not yet in `PAPER_FACTS`. Until a before/after confusion sub-matrix is reported, the abstract must soften to "revisits" / "reduces one direction of" and the conclusion must not repeat "disproving". Likewise "raises recall by 4.4 points … at fixed precision" implies a matched operating point; precision moved 70.9→71.2 % at the same threshold (conf 0.25), so write "at a fixed threshold and essentially unchanged precision", as Sec. 1 already does correctly.
2. **The paper is ~55 % skeleton; the central evidence for RQ1 and half of RQ2 does not exist yet.** Sec. 3 has no text and `tab:rq1` has six empty cells; Sec. 4 has one equation and six `\TODO`s; Sec. 5.1 (setup), 5.2 (E0/E10 mean±std, E14, E7, paired test), 5.4 and 5.5 are TODO; Sec. 7 is one TODO. As written, RQ1 — the first contribution and the "data first" half of the title — has zero supporting evidence, and the abstract still promises "each mapped to a documented failure and ablated individually". Either the ablation lands or RQ1 must be demoted from contribution to protocol description. This is not a writing nit; a submission with unfilled `\TODO` red text is a desk reject, and silently hiding them with the commented-out `\renewcommand{\TODO}[1]{}` in preamble.tex:39 would leave logically empty sections.
3. **Inaccurate statement in contribution 4 (sec/1_intro.tex, contribution 4).** "multi-scale training at 1920 px … all fail to beat the simple baseline" — per `PAPER_FACTS` §1/§4, E13 hit CUDA OOM and was *not run*; it cannot have "failed to beat" anything. Move it to the "not attempted" sentence already present in Sec. 6 ("Method scope") and drop it from the negative-results list, or rephrase as "was infeasible in 80 GB".
4. **Species acronym collision with the prior paper — a real risk of factual error in Secs. 3/5/Supp.** In the PACBB paper (see `paper/_scratch/pacbb.txt`, l. 164–166, 354–357) **MF = Flesh Fly** and BF = Blow Fly, FF = Fruit Fly, HF = House Fly. In this draft and `PAPER_FACTS` **MF = fruit fly**, MC = flesh fly, MV = blow fly, MD = house fly. The supplementary skeleton (sec/X_suppl.tex, "Error analysis MF↔BF / MD↔MV") already mixes the two vocabularies, and the Sec. 5.3 TODO "MF 78.3→84.4" refers to fruit fly, which a reader of the cited paper will read as flesh fly. Expand every species acronym once in Sec. 3.1 (MD/MV/MC/MF with common and, ideally, Latin family names), never use BF/FF/HF, and when paraphrasing the prior paper's "irreducible" pair say "flesh fly/blow fly" in words (Sec. 1 P2 does this correctly).
5. **Teaser figure is the template placeholder (fig/teaser.tex).** Three `example-image-golden` images with the author-kit caption ("example of a 2-column teaser…") occupy the top of page 1. Either supply a real teaser (e.g. a 1920×1080 plate with a 40 px crop inset, the three plate types, and the recall/mAP paradox) or delete the `\input{fig/teaser}` line. As is, it is the first thing a reviewer sees.
6. **Table `tab:rq3` overflows the column (main.log: "Overfull \hbox (59.1 pt too wide)" for sec/5_experiments.tex ll. 35–47); `tab:rq1` (+14.0 pt) and `tab:rq2` (+4.9 pt) also overflow.** 59 pt is ~0.8 in past the 3¼ in column into the gutter/neighbouring column; this will be visible in print. Use `\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{…}`, abbreviate the header ("+ CNX-T parts"), or move latency to a footnote.
7. **`\bibitem` note "VERIFY series/volume/pages once the proceedings are published (accepted Sept. 2026, to appear)" (main.bib, `pacbb2026datacentric`) is rendered in the PDF reference list.** Remove the `note` before submission. More importantly, the whole argument (three failure modes, the "irreducible" quote, 0.476→0.608) rests on a paper that may not be publicly accessible at the CVPR deadline; if the proceedings are not out, reviewers cannot verify any of it. Ensure a public version exists (arXiv/tech report is allowed) or reproduce the essential facts (the quoted sentence, Table 4 numbers) in the supplementary.
8. **Dataset-as-contribution is undecided and the text currently hedges in both directions.** Abstract: "release code \TODO{and data, if approved}"; Sec. 6: "if they cannot, the contribution of Sec. 3 is the acquisition protocol and the code". CVPR policy: a dataset listed as a contribution must be public by camera-ready. Contribution 1 currently reads as new *data* ("Three new acquisition protocols … plus a copy-paste augmentation"). Decide, and make abstract, contribution 1, Sec. 3.1 and Sec. 7 say the same thing.

## 6. Justification of Rating
The parts that exist — introduction, related work and limitations — are well argued, honestly hedged and correctly positioned against tiny-object detection, data-centric vision, FGVC and insect-monitoring work, with the prior paper in third person. However, the manuscript is currently a scaffold: the RQ1 section and table are empty, the RQ2 table has no multi-seed entries, setup, negative results and conclusion are TODOs, the teaser is the template placeholder, and the abstract carries an overclaim ("disproving the irreducibility claim") that the available per-species numbers do not support (flesh-fly recall actually decreases). Several format issues (a 59 pt overfull table, a "VERIFY…" note in the reference list, red `\TODO` text) would each trigger desk rejection if left in. None of these are hard to fix once the pending jobs finish, and the honest Limitations section suggests the final paper could land at 3–4 if the RQ1 ablation is completed and the abstract is toned down.
