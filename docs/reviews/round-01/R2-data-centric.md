## 1. Paper Summary

The paper continues a previously published data-centric pipeline for per-species fly counting on industrial adhesive traps (four target species, ≈40 px objects in native 1920×1080 frames). Its data-side claim (RQ1) is that three acquisition protocols designed against residual failures documented by the prior work — same-species crowding plates (intra-species occlusion), graduated plates (scale variance) and blank negative plates (background false positives) — plus a copy-paste augmentation that transplants *real* annotated instances between training plates, improve detection more than volume-driven synthetic rendering, with each acquisition to be ablated individually and jointly (Tab. 1). Its model-side claims are a hybrid CIoU–NWD objective inside an end-to-end YOLO26 (RQ2, +1.4 pp VAL mAP50, single seed) and a detector→crop→ConvNeXt-T cascade that raises species recall on a 55-image test set from 75.7 % to 80.1 % while lowering 7-class mAP50 by 4.7 pp (RQ3, Tab. 3). At this version, RQ1 has no experimental evidence at all, the base dataset (1,259/35/55 images, 7 classes) is described only inside a TODO, the split rule is asserted but marked "confirm", and whether the data will be released is undecided.

## 2. Strengths

**S1 — The RQ1 design principle is the right one and is clearly articulated (Sec. 1 P3–P4-1, Sec. 2 ¶2).** Mapping each new plate type to one *documented* failure of the previous dataset generation, rather than maximising volume or generic diversity, is a genuinely data-centric experimental design, and the intended ablation (Tab. 1: base / +each acquisition / +all / +all+copy-paste, same detector recipe, mean±std over seeds) is exactly the protocol this reviewer would ask for. If executed as specified, it would be one of the few detection papers with a per-failure attribution of data interventions.

**S2 — The copy-paste positioning is honest about prior art (Sec. 2 ¶2).** The paper cites Dwibedi et al. (ICCV 2017) and Ghiasi et al. (CVPR 2021), does not claim copy-paste as new, and frames the contribution as the *domain* argument — real instances from the deployment camera carry the adhesive texture, specular residue and lighting that rendered insects lack. The TODO in Sec. 3.4 already fixes a train-only source/target policy, which is the correct leakage rule.

**S3 — The limitations section already contains most of what a data-centric reviewer looks for (Sec. 6).** Single site / single camera / single adhesive type (¶1), the "consolidated, not blind" status of the test set with the ensemble explicitly demoted to exploratory (¶2), classifier seed variance of several F1 points and an explicit refusal to interpret 1–2 pp differences on 617/918 instances (¶3), two evaluators kept apart (¶4), and — importantly for this reviewer — a pre-emptive reframing: "if [the images] cannot [be released], the contribution of Sec. 3 is the acquisition protocol and the code, not the dataset itself" (¶1). This is the correct reading of the CVPR dataset-contribution policy and should be weighed positively.

**S4 — The prior work is treated as third-party and its numbers are reproduced faithfully (Sec. 1 P2, Sec. 2 ¶2, ¶4).** 0.476→0.608 mAP50, YOLO26-S held fixed, >2,700 rendered images that failed to transfer, the "irreducible boundary" quote and the single-seed admission all match `PAPER_FACTS.md` §5. The CVPR draft does not reuse tables, figures or prose from that paper (nothing to reuse yet in Sec. 3, see W6).

**S5 — Every number that is written matches `PAPER_FACTS.md`.** Checked: 44.4/39.2 px and 0.1 % of the frame (44.4²/(1920·1080)=0.095 %); 0.857/0.871/0.864/0.672/0.862/0.626; Tab. 3 all cells; 55 images / 2,864 boxes / 918 species; 617 val species; 70.1–72.6 % F1; 0.857 vs 0.812; 416.5/22.9 and 463/25 ms; 4,197 proposals.

## 3. Weaknesses

**Major (must fix before submission)**

1. **RQ1 is presented as a result in the title, abstract and contribution list, but there is no evidence of any kind (Sec. 0; Sec. 1 P4-1; Sec. 3; Tab. 1; Sec. 5.4).** The abstract states the acquisitions are "each mapped to a documented failure and ablated individually"; Tab. 1 has every cell empty; Sec. 6 ¶5 says the ablation "is not yet run"; and `PAPER_FACTS.md` §0/§7-1 records that the authors do not yet know *whether the new plates are inside DS-F2_v8.1.1 at all*, nor whether any model has been trained with or without them. This last point is critical for the ablation design: if the crowding/graduated/blank plates are already in the 1,259 training images used by E0 and E10, then the "Base" row of Tab. 1 is not the current baseline, the ablation must be a *leave-one-out removal*, and every RQ2/RQ3 number in the paper is already "with" the new data. The paper must (a) state the counts of each plate type in train/val/test, (b) say which experiments include them, and (c) either run Tab. 1 or remove RQ1 from the title, abstract and contribution list. As written, the first-listed contribution is unsupported.

2. **The "real copy-paste beats synthetic rendering" and "pixel fidelity, not volume" claims are not testable with the described design (Sec. 0; Sec. 1 P3 RQ1; Sec. 2 ¶2; Sec. 3.4).** The Blender failure was observed by the prior work on a *different dataset generation* (STID Raw, `PAPER_FACTS` §5), with a different detector (YOLO26-S) and evaluator; the copy-paste result will be measured on DS-F2 with YOLO26m. Comparing the two across datasets confounds data source with base data, volume, detector and evaluator, and would only allow the inference "our augmentation helped, theirs did not" — not "fidelity matters more than volume". A controlled comparison needs, on the same base data and recipe: (i) real-instance copy-paste at volume $N$; (ii) rendered instances pasted onto the *same real plates* at volume $N$ (this isolates instance fidelity from background/context fidelity, which copy-paste also supplies for free); and ideally (iii) real-instance copy-paste at a volume matched to the >2,700 rendered images. Without at least (i)+(ii), the abstract's contrast with synthetic data must be weakened to "consistent with", and the hypothesis presented as a hypothesis. Note also that `fly_det/config/yolo.yaml` sets `copy_paste: 0.0` and `PAPER_FACTS` §7-2 has no run name or number for the copy-paste experiment (E9); the paper must state whether the augmentation is the Ultralytics segment-level copy-paste or a custom box-level transplant, the source-instance policy (which classes, minimum size, occlusion handling, blending or none as in Ghiasi et al.), and the number of pasted instances per image. Prior art specific to *small-object* copy-paste should also be cited: Kisantal et al., "Augmentation for small object detection" (arXiv:1902.07296, 2019) oversample and paste small instances precisely to fix the small-object recall gap; the paper's contribution relative to that must be stated.

3. **Split provenance is asserted in the text but unconfirmed in the facts file, and the available project documentation suggests the split is by *collection version*, not by plate (Sec. 6 ¶2; Sec. 3.1 TODO; Sec. 5.1 TODO).** Sec. 6 ¶2 states "Train/val/test are separated by plate and acquisition period … no plate appears in two splits", while `PAPER_FACTS` §0 and §7-3 mark the rule as "confirm", and the project's planning notes record that the split "came ready-made by collection version (DS-F2_v5.1/v6.0/v7/v8 + DS-ORG)" with plate disjointness still an open checkbox. A version-based split has two implications the paper must address: (a) plate disjointness must be *verified* (same plate photographed in two sessions, or cropped/re-shot, would land in two splits) and the verification method stated; (b) if val/test come from specific later versions, there is a train/test *distribution shift by design* (different acquisition SOP, camera settings, season), and the 35-image validation set (2.6 % of images, 617 species instances) used for *all* model selection may not be representative of train. The paper must also state where the three new plate types land: if crowding/graduated/blank plates appear in val/test, the evaluation partly measures fit to staged plates rather than to deployment traps; if they appear only in train, say so and say why. A statement of the form "split by plate ID; $k_\text{train}/k_\text{val}/k_\text{test}$ plates from $p$ acquisition periods; verified by …" is required, and a datasheet-style summary (Gebru et al., "Datasheets for datasets", CACM 2021) in the supplementary would settle most of Sec. 3.1.

4. **Dataset-as-contribution status must be decided before submission, and the current text is inconsistent (Sec. 0 last sentence; Sec. 1 P4-1; Sec. 3.1 TODO; Sec. 6 ¶1; Sec. 7 TODO).** The abstract promises to "release code \TODO{and data, if approved}", the title leads with "From Better Data", and contribution 1 is the acquisition itself. CVPR policy is unambiguous: a dataset claimed as a contribution must be public by camera-ready. Sec. 6 ¶1 already gives the correct fallback (protocol + code). The authors must pick one path now: either obtain the partner's written release commitment and name the dataset publicly (the internal name "DS-F2_v8.1.1" is not a public name), or rewrite contribution 1 as "three acquisition *protocols* and a copy-paste *procedure*, released as code and documentation", and strike "and data" from the abstract and conclusion. Under the second path, Tab. 1 remains valid as evidence for the protocols; under either path, the relation to STID (is DS-F2 a superset of STID SOP? are any STID images in DS-F2 val/test?) must be stated because the prior work's numbers are quoted three times.

5. **Class definitions and the mapping to the prior work's taxonomy are missing, and RQ3's target pair depends on them (Sec. 1 P1–P2; Sec. 1 P4-3; Sec. 6 ¶1; `PAPER_FACTS` §5).** The prior work used six classes (FF/HF/BF/MF/OI/Noise) and declared the "MF↔BF" confusion irreducible; the present paper uses seven (MD/MV/MC/MF/INS/NOISE/MAR) and translates the irreducible pair as "flesh fly / blow fly". `PAPER_FACTS` §5 itself says "STID ≠ DS-F2 — confirm relation/class mapping before comparing numbers". A class-mapping table (prior code → present code → common name → taxonomic rank) is mandatory: without it a reader cannot verify that the pair the cascade is claimed to fix is the pair the prior work called irreducible. Two further data-quality points must be written into Sec. 3.1 / Sec. 6 ¶1: (a) the four "species" are at mixed taxonomic ranks — *house fly* is a species (*Musca domestica*), *blow fly* (Calliphoridae) and *flesh fly* (Sarcophagidae) are families, and *fruit fly* is ambiguous between Drosophilidae and Tephritidae, which differ in size by a factor that matters at 40 px; the paper must say what the annotators actually labelled and whether an entomologist verified the class definitions and a sample of labels (Sec. 6 ¶1 TODO); (b) the project's internal notes record uncertainty about what class code MC denotes — if the team was unsure, the reader will be too. Inter-annotator agreement on a held-out subset, or at least the annotation guideline for occluded specimens in crowding plates (amodal vs. visible-part box), is needed because the crowding acquisition specifically produces the boxes hardest to annotate consistently.

6. **Non-target classes (INS/NOISE/MAR) are handled inconsistently across metrics, and their share of the data is large enough to matter (Tab. 3; Sec. 6 ¶4; Sec. 5.1 TODO).** In val and test, non-target classes are ≈63 % of all boxes (1,649 − 617 and 2,864 − 918). Tab. 3 mixes 4-species P/R/F1 with 7-class mAP50 in the same table, and the recall/precision definitions are not given: is a species GT matched (class-agnostic IoU ≥ 0.5) to an INS or NOISE prediction a miss or a wrong-species error? Is a species prediction matched to a NOISE GT a false positive in the 70.9 % precision? Does MAR (printed grid/markers) count as an object class in mAP50, and if so, how much of the 76.8 %→72.1 % drop is MAR/NOISE AP moving? These definitions must be in Sec. 5.1, with a 7-row per-class table in Sec. 5.3 / Suppl. §2 (already planned). The interaction with RQ1 is direct: graduated plates add printed scale marks (more MAR-like objects) and blank plates add pure background (no boxes, or only MAR boxes?) — the paper must state how each new plate type is annotated with respect to MAR and NOISE, and how many label-free "background images" enter training (Ultralytics guidance is 0–10 %; above that, recall on positive plates can fall).

7. **The mechanism linking each acquisition to its failure is not yet argued, and for graduated plates it is not obvious (Sec. 3.3; Sec. 5.4 TODO).** With a fixed camera at a fixed working distance (Sec. 1 P1), where does *scale variance between plates* come from, and how does printing a scale on the plate help a detector that is forbidden to rescale the image? If the intent is scale *diversity* (photographing graduated plates at several distances) or a per-plate metric reference used downstream, say so; if the printed scale merely allows *measuring* the variance, it is a diagnostic tool rather than a data fix. For crowding plates, the paper must say whether the plates are *staged* (specimens placed by hand) or natural catches; staged plates change specimen posture, adhesive spread and class balance, which is a source of dataset shift the paper should acknowledge. For blank plates, "pure background" must be defined (unused adhesive? used adhesive with debris but no insects?) since the documented failure is debris mimicking the smallest species, which a pristine blank plate would not contain. The planned per-failure evidence (Sec. 5.4 TODO: "crowding → MD recall? blank → NOISE FPs?") is the right diagnostic; it must be *pre-specified* (which metric, which class) before Tab. 1 is filled, otherwise the attribution is post hoc.

**Minor**

1. **Validation set size (35 images, 617 species instances) is used for every selection decision in the paper — best epoch, classifier choice, mixing weight $w$, GCD vs. NWD — yet Sec. 6 ¶3 only discusses it as a noise floor.** State it as a selection-bias risk (optimistic VAL numbers in Tab. 1–2) and consider reporting Tab. 1 on TEST as well, since RQ1 has not touched TEST yet and could be the one truly blind result in the paper.
2. **The title over-promises relative to the evidence ("From Better Data to a Better Model").** Read together with 0.476→0.608 (prior) and 0.857 (E0 here), it invites the inference that the present data doubled mAP — but the two numbers come from different datasets, detectors and evaluators. Add one sentence in Sec. 3.1 stating explicitly that these numbers are not comparable.
3. **Sec. 1 P1 "the client forbids resizing the image"** — fine for anonymity, but it is a *constraint*, not a *finding*; the paper should say whether it was ever tested that downscaling hurts (it is the standard practitioner escape and readers will ask).
4. **Sec. 6 ¶1 lists site/camera/adhesive as limitations but not temporal coverage** (seasons, insect population dynamics), nor the fact that all three new plate types are artificial constructs that will not appear in deployment traps. Add both.
5. **Fig. 1 is a placeholder.** For a data-centric paper, the teaser should show one crop each of the three failure modes on real plates and the corresponding new plate type; this is more informative than any architecture diagram.
6. **Supplementary §3–§4 are the natural place for a datasheet** (composition, collection process, preprocessing, uses, distribution, maintenance) and for copy-paste examples; both are TODO.

## 4. Questions for the authors

Q1. Are the crowding, graduated and blank plates already inside the 1,259 training images used for E0/E10? If yes, how many of each, and is Tab. 1 planned as an add-in or a leave-one-out ablation?

Q2. How exactly is plate identity tracked (plate ID in filename/metadata?), and has plate disjointness across train/val/test been *verified*, not assumed? How many distinct plates and acquisition periods does each split contain?

Q3. Do any of the new plate types appear in val or test? If so, what fraction of the 617/918 species instances come from them?

Q4. For the copy-paste augmentation: Ultralytics built-in or custom? Source classes, size filter, blending, pasted instances per image, and total augmented images? Are source instances drawn only from train plates and pasted only onto train plates, with val/test never touched?

Q5. Will the partner allow public release of the images? If not, will the acquisition protocol (camera, distance, illumination, plate templates for the graduated/crowding plates) and the copy-paste code be released so the protocol is reproducible on another site?

Q6. Provide the class mapping between the prior work's FF/HF/BF/MF/OI/Noise and the present MD/MV/MC/MF/INS/NOISE/MAR, and confirm which pair the prior work called irreducible in the present nomenclature.

Q7. Who annotated the species labels, at what taxonomic rank, and was any subset double-annotated or checked by an entomologist? What is the box convention for partially occluded specimens in crowding plates?

Q8. In Tab. 3, how are species-GT↔INS/NOISE matches and species-prediction↔NOISE-GT matches counted in recall and precision? How many "background" (label-free) images enter training once blank plates are added?

## 6. Justification of Rating

From a data-centric standpoint the paper's *plan* is strong: failure-targeted acquisition with per-failure ablation (S1), an honest positioning of copy-paste against Dwibedi/Ghiasi (S2), a limitations section that already anticipates the dataset-release question and the test-set status (S3), and every written number consistent with the facts file (S5). But the paper's first-named contribution has no evidence (W1), the synthetic-vs-real claim is not testable with the described design (W2), the split rule is asserted rather than verified and appears to be version-based (W3), the dataset-as-contribution decision is unresolved while the title and abstract lean on it (W4), and the class taxonomy that RQ3 hinges on is not mapped to the prior work (W5). None of this is unfixable: W1 and W2 need the ablation runs plus one control; W3–W6 need a paragraph each of verified facts; W7 needs an argued rationale. If Tab. 1 is filled with multi-seed numbers, the split is verified by plate, and the contribution is reframed as protocol + code unless the data are released, the data side would move to Accept territory. At this version it cannot be recommended.
