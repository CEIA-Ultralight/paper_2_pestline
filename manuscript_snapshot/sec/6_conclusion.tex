\section{\uppercase{Conclusions}}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked what limits species-level reading of sticky traps and which lever moves that limit. Along a ladder of five cumulative training sets, species accuracy rises from 56.1\% to 82.2\%, with the largest gains on objects below 32\,px and insect-only plates and part of the look-alike confusion shrinking; but random subsamples of the final training set of the size of L1 and L2 reach the same accuracy, so up to L2 the rise is explained mostly by the number of images, not by targeting. Identification did not move with the classifier: with our recipe, a detect-then-classify cascade does not improve on the single-stage detector in any of 15 configurations, ground-truth-crop accuracy saturates near 83\% for five classifiers (four backbones), and the residual errors are confident and shared by all classifiers, which is consistent with limited information for MD/MV/MC, though label noise or the crop recipe cannot be excluded. Tiny-object methods from aerial imagery bring no gain detectable here: none of eight variants improves species accuracy over a same-campaign control with three seeds (all differences between $-3.8$ and $+0.3$\,pp except NWD with RFLA at $-8.8$\,pp), and none raises the recall of objects below 32\,px. For practitioners of trap-based monitoring, our results suggest that collecting more annotated plates matters more than model complexity (whether targeting adds beyond quantity remains open), and investigating the remaining confusion within the housefly/blowfly/flesh-fly family as a possible labeling and imaging problem, which expert re-annotation could settle.

\paragraph{Limitations.} One facility and one test split (73 images; 7 crowded and 6 insect-only plates); three seeds; the equal-size control is partial (three seeds at 244 images, two at 869, none at 103); the cascade experiments use the final dataset only. Cross-trap-colour evaluation~\cite{ong2025} was not done; the split rule by plate is not documented, so leakage between images of the same plate cannot be excluded; RQ3 ran on a different GPU than RQ1/RQ2; checkpoints were selected on VAL, so no never-consulted hold-out exists. Near-duplicate frames may inflate scores. The tiny-object variants used default hyperparameters without per-variant tuning, so the result is ``no gain detected here'', not proof that they cannot help, and the NWD+RFLA drop stems from unstable seeds. Ground-truth-crop accuracy near 83\% cannot separate ambiguous pixels from label noise. Generalization beyond this facility is untested and the code and configurations will be released but the images will not, so the ladder cannot be reproduced independently.

\paragraph{Future work.} Out-of-distribution rejection for novel species~\cite{opensect2025}, expert re-annotation of the confusable pairs, a complete equal-size control, edge deployment.
