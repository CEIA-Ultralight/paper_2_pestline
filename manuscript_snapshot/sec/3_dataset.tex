\section{\uppercase{The DS-F2 Dataset}}
\label{sec:dataset}

\subsection{Lineage and Acquisition Protocol}
\label{sec:dataset:lineage}

DS-F2 was built from successive plate batches v5.1, v6.0, v7, v8 and v9, photographed at $1920\times1080$ (industrial sticky traps at a fixed focal distance). A batch is a collection of original images; the evaluated version, DS-F2\_v9.0.5, combines all five with annotations, splits and augmentation. The earlier v4 collection of the prior pipeline~\cite{pacbb2026} uses $1280\times720$ images and six classes; the recorded identity checks found no image in common between v4 and the later collections, so v4 is not a subset of DS-F2.

\subsection{Targeted Physical Collections}
\label{sec:dataset:collections}

The original plate-image batches (before augmentation) hold 97 (v5.1), 83 (v6.0), 70 (v7), 100 (v8) and 174 (v9) images; the maximum number of target instances in one image is 42, 35, 34, 75 and 77.

\paragraph{Composition.}
Batch v5.1 has mixed-species plates, v6.0 both kinds, later batches species-pure plates; handwritten plate identifiers associate D, V, C, F with MD, MV, MC, MF and G with INS. INS-only plates (no target species) appear in v7, v8 and v9 (8, 20 and 40 images) to reduce false assignments to the target classes. A printed $8\times4$ grid is present in all batches, including v4, so it does not distinguish the new collections.

\paragraph{White-paper photographs and augmentation.}
DS-ORG (flies on white paper, one species per photograph, about 58 instances per image) is in versions v5.3.5--v8.1.1 and absent from v9.0.5, which introduced instance copy-paste of real annotated crops. Generated images are not in the batch counts, which therefore differ from the RQ1 training counts.

\subsection{Classes and Statistics}
\label{sec:dataset:classes}

The detector uses seven classes: four target fly species, MD (housefly, \emph{mosca doméstica}), MV (blowfly, \emph{mosca varejeira}), MC (flesh fly, \emph{mosca da carne}) and MF (fruit fly, \emph{mosca da fruta}), and three non-target classes, INS (other insects), NOISE and MAR. The evaluated version has 1{,}738 training images (88{,}128 boxes of all classes), 52 validation images (856 species instances) and 73 test images (1{,}210 species instances: MD 224, MV 302, MC 268, MF 416). The mean object size is $\sqrt{wh}\approx44$\,px in $1920\times1080$ images. Validation and test images share no file name or hash with training, but the dataset records no plate identifier per image, so we cannot exclude that images of the same plate appear on both sides of a split; we return to this in the limitations.
